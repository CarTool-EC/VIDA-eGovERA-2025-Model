Goals
Mission: Taxation and Customs deliver on the Union’s priorities and provide revenues to fund European investment and growth.
Tax related Vision: Taxation and customs has an essential role in improving the functioning of the Single Market. The European Commission presents proposals for tax legislation. Infringement policy aims to enforce the proper application of EU tax legislation in all Member States.
General Objective 1: European Green Deal
Strategic goal: Implement the Digital Reporting Requirements imposed by the VAT in the Digital Age package
Specific objective 3.2: Tax policy actions for more fairness and social justice
Specific objective 3.1: Tax policy actions for strong, fair and efficient Single Market
General Objective 3: An economy that works for the people
Specific Objective 1.1: EU tax policy actions for building a carbon neutral continent
