Quantity and nature of goods/services ()
Definition: Quantity and nature of the goods supplied or the extent and nature of the services rendered. Where one of the special arrangements applicable to second-hand goods, works of art, collectors’ items and antiques is applied, mention ‘Margin scheme — Second-hand goods’; ‘Margin scheme — Works of art’ or ‘Margin scheme — Collector’s items and antiques’ respectively. In the case of the supply of a new means of transport made in accordance with the conditions specified in Article 138(1) and (2)(a), the characteristics as identified in point (b) of Article 2(2) of the Directive 2006/112/EC, clarify the nature of means.
