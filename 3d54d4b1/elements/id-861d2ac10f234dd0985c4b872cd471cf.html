Fiscalis Programme goals ()
10.40 Goals detail view
Fiscalis Programme goals Support tax authorities and taxation to enhance the functioning of the internal market
Fiscalis Programme goals Improve tax collection and protect the Union and its Member States from tax fraud, tax evasion and tax avoidance, among other financial and economic interests
Fiscalis Programme goals Support the development of tax policy actions and the implementation of Union law relating to taxation
Fiscalis Programme goals Foster the competitiveness of the Union and fair competition in the Union
Fiscalis Programme goals Foster cooperation between tax authorities, including exchange of tax information and capacity building development such as human competency and European electronic systems
