Description: EXECUTIVE VICE-PRESIDENT (2024-2029) | Tech Sovereignty, Security and Democracy https://commission.europa.eu/about/organisation/college-commissioners/henna-virkkunen_en Current position by : Henna Virkkunen As Executive Vice-President for Tech Sovereignty, Security and Democracy, Henna Virkkunen is tasked with combatting the increasingly complex security threats facing the EU, whilst at the same time strengthening our external borders and our internal security. This goes hand in hand with her responsibility to ensure our freedoms, justice and democracy are strengthened too. Henna Virkkunen is also responsible for the Digital and Frontier Technologies portfolio. This entails leading Europe’s efforts in shaping a competitive, resilient and inclusive digital future and maintaining or attaining leadership in strategic digital technologies.
