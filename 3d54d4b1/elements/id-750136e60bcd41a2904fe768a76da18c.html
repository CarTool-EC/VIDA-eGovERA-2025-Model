DG TAXUD ()
Definition: TAXUD (DG Taxation and Customs Union) is the European Commission Directorate-General responsible for developing, implementing, and monitoring EU tax and customs policies. Its mandate includes ensuring the smooth functioning of the internal market, safeguarding revenues that finance the EU budget, facilitating legitimate trade, preventing fraud and tax evasion, and promoting cooperation among Member States’ administrations. In the context of ArchiMate, TAXUD is represented at the business layer, performing organizational processes and functions that align EU-level taxation and customs objectives. Source: EIRA team Additional information: TAXUD plays a central role in ensuring that EU taxation and customs frameworks are coherent, harmonized, and effective across all Member States.
