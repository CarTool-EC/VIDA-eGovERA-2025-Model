Viva la ViDA! ()
The EU policy concerning ViDA is a major driver to implement changes in the way information about cross-border B2B transactions is collected and processed in the EU member states. The Focal Public Administration, the National Tax Administration of the member state is responsible for implementing these changes. Source: https://taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu/news/viva-la-vida-2024-11-28_en Description: " It is the biggest VAT reform since the Single Market. It is indeed a wide reform package proposed by the European Commission in 2022 to modernise and simplify the EU’s VAT (Value Added Tax) systems. In short, ViDA aims to create a more efficient and digital system for VAT obligations, while reducing administrative burdens, and tackling tax fraud. ViDA also addresses challenges that we see with increased online trade and it helps level the playing field between platforms and traditional providers in the short-term accommodation rental and passenger transport sectors."
