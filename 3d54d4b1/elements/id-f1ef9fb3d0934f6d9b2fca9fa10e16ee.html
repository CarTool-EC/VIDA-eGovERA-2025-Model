Description: Tax related Vision: Taxation and customs has an essential role in improving the functioning of the Single Market. The European Commission presents proposals for tax legislation where it considers that EU-wide action is needed. Infringement policy aims to enforce the proper application of EU tax and customs legislation in all Member States and to remove distortions of competition. Source: DG TAXUD Strategic plan 2020-2024 https://commission.europa.eu/publications/strategic-plan-2020-2024-taxation-and-customs-union_en
