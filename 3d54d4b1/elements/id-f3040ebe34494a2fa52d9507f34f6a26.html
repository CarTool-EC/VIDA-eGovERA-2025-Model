Requirements ()
Description: The [Requirements] subgrouping of the [Motivation] grouping contains the requirements and constraints that are to be applied at organisation level in the [Focal Public Administration]. They help to realise the [Outcomes]. A Requirement represents a statement of need defining a property that applies to a specific system as described by the architecture. Requirements can reflect specific needs of certain [Stakeholders] and/or specify desired [Outcomes] of strategic goals. The [Public Policy Context] and [Digital Solution Requirement] EIRA ABB have been included here as examples that may be included in the enterprise architecture of the [Focal Public Administration]. Note : The "Digital Solution Non-Functional Requirements Catalogue" viewpoint in the 'eGovera Business Agnostic RA v6.1.0' (https://interoperable-europe.ec.europa.eu/collection/european-interoperability-reference-architecture-eira/solution/egovera/releases) includes a set of example Requirements ABB (requirements) that could be considered for integration in the enterprise architecture of the [Focal Public Administration]. They describe how the solution should perform (rather than what it should do).
