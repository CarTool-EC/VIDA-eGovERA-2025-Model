Information, Human, Financial and Physical resources ()
Description: A Resource represents an asset owned or controlled by the [Focal Public Administration] that can be assigned to one or more [Capabilities]. Examples include information resources, for example documents and data and the processes that deliver them; human resources; financial resources and physical resources like buildings and equipment.
