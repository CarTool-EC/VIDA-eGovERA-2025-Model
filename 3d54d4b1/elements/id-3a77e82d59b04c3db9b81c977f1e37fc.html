Outcomes ()
Description: The [Outcomes] subgrouping of the [Movtivation] grouping contains the [Outcomes] that are realised by the [Focal Public Administration]. The [Outcomes] are realised by applying the [Requirements] (potentially regulated by some [Constraints] and the [Principles] that are specified in the motivation view. [Outcomes], on their turn, realise the [Strategic goals] of the [Focal Public Administration], and in this way create the [Public Value] for the [Stakeholders]. On a strategic level, outcomes are realised as a result of a [Public policy], i.e. a management decision to create or improve a value creating public administration service. This [Public policy] in its turn is realised by deploying a [Business capability]. Note: The EIRA [Interoperable European Solution] outcome ABB from the EIRA [Enterprise Architecture Framework alignment guidelines] view may be aggregated in one of the [Outcome] ABB of the [Focal Public Administration]. Note: [Strategic goals] in themselves do not create value for the [Stakeholders]. It is the [Outcomes] that create value.
