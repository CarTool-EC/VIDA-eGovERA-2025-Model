Ethical values ()
Description: The [Ethical values] subgroup of the [Motivation] grouping contains the most important Ethical values of the [Focal Public Administration]. They are modeled as principles, because they are guidelines that hold true for all operations of the [Focal Public Administration]. Examples : "People at the centre", "Freedom of choice", "Safe and Secure", "Solidarity and Inclusion".
