Data Inmutability ()
Description: eInvoice data related to the Digital Reporting Requirements (DRRs) remains unalterable after issuance, preserving integrity, compliance, and auditability across borders. Detailed description.Once an eInvoice is created and transmitted, its core data elements (i.e., DRRs) cannot be modified or overwritten. Any correction or adjustment is handled through new documents, maintaining a complete and verifiable history.
