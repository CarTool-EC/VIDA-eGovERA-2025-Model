Digital business capability ()
A [Digital business capability] is the ability of the [Focal Public Administration] to deliver a digital service to the public. They are aggregated into the more generic [Business capability] In the context of the development of a European Interoperable Digital Public Service, this capability corresponds to the "Digital Business Capability" ABB that can be found in EIRA and eGovERA reference architectures: http://data.europa.eu/dr8/DigitalBusinessCapabilityCapability
