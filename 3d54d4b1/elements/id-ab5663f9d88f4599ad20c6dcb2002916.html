Interoperable Europe Act requirements ()
Requirements that result from the Interoperable Europe Act (Regulation 2022/0379 on laying down measures for a high level of public sector interoperability across the Union) Source: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A52022PC0720. These requirements are a statement of need of DG Digit, a major stakeholder that is supporting the Focal Public Administration in the realisation of the ViDA package.
