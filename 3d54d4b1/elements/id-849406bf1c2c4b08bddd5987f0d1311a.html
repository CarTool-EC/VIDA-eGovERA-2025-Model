Architecture Principle ()
A principle defines a general property that applies to any system in a certain context, whereas a requirement defines a property that applies to a specific system as described by an architecture. The [Architecture Principle] ABB has been included here as a placeholder for defining the architecture principles that the Focal Public Adminstration wants to apply in their enterprise architecture. Note, the ‘Architecture Principles’ viewpoint in the 'eGovera Business Agnostic RA v6.1.0' (https://interoperable-europe.ec.europa.eu/collection/european-interoperability-reference-architecture-eira/solution/egovera/releases) provides a valuable overview of architecture principle ABBs that could be considered for integration in the enterprise architecture of the [Focal Public Administration].
