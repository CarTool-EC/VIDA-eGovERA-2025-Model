Deemed supplier in platform economy (2027-01-01) ()
Description: Platforms in transport and short term accommodation sectors are to account for the VAT rather than underlying supplier. The elements of Directive 2006/112/EC which require amendments to Implementing Regulation (EU) No 282/2011 are to be transposed by Member States into national legislation by 30 June 2028. Therefore, it is necessary for the amendments to that Regulation to be applicable from 1 July 2028.
