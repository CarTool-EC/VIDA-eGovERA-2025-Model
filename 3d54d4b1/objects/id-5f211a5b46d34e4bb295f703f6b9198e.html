«Focal Public Administration» ()
Description: The PAAF Focal Public Administration group ABB contains the Public Administration ABB that is modelled in this Enterprise Architecture model. All other ABB in this model have to be interpreted in the context of the Focal Public Administration, that is the central unit of analysis. If the Focal Public Administration is part of a larger Public Administration, this can be indicated by including a Public Administration ABB with a composition or aggregation relationship. Occasionally, the Enterprise Architecture will document more than one Public Administration. In this case, this should also be reflected by including more than one Public Administration ABB in this group.
